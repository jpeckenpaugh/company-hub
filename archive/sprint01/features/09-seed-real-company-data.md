# Feature: Seed Real Company Data

## Capability

Seed the application with real companies rather than fictitious ones: one of the
biggest players in each seeded industry (Manufacturing → Toyota Motor (JP),
Technology → Samsung Electronics (KR), Finance → HSBC (UK), Healthcare →
Novartis (CH), Energy → Shell (UK), Retail → Carrefour (FR)). Each seeded
company carries one Headquarters location (label, city, country, type)
consistent with the location model. The seed is limited to these six companies'
structured fields and their Headquarters locations; no references, news, logos,
or artifacts are seeded.
